Landscape sound credits

Landscape ambience uses recorded and synthesized source samples, plus original procedural cues. Runtime modifications: excerpts up to 40 or 150 seconds, DC removal, volume normalization, filtering, playback-rate changes, stereo positioning, fades, randomized excerpts, irregular clusters and layering. Freesound assets use publicly playable previews capped at their leading 3 MB. These sounds represent the scenery, not authentic recordings of the named places. Sather uses church tower bells as a Campanile-style approximation. The winter bird is a black-capped chickadee used as a scenic sound-design reference.
