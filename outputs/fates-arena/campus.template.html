Campus field guide

Maja Kaminska · Hold click to shoot. Q: Tank Brigade boards a 240 HP tank. Click inside its 560-unit range to fire 95-radius shells. Incoming damage hits the tank; destroying it ejects Maja without spillover damage. Space: Firing Squad summons five UN 5th Army soldiers for three forward volleys. E: Command summons an 8-second rifle escort; press E again and click an enemy to focus it. R: Brilliant Tactician lets you place a flag within 440 units. Allies within its 320-unit aura deal 1.5× damage and take 0.9× damage for 12 seconds. Right click cancels targeting.

Explore Sproul Plaza, Sather Gate, Memorial Glade, West Crescent, and the Campanile. The minimap shows the whole campus. A gameplay interpretation, not a navigation map. Green crosses restore health. You respawn after 4 seconds. Friendly attacks pass through teammates.

Move WASD / arrows · Attack hold left click · Evade Shift · Pause Escape

1 / Q · Sail Abroad — Ride a boat for 3 seconds. It steers toward your cursor, with a flowing wake and contact damage. 7s cooldown.
2 / Space · Tidal Wave — Send a fast, broad wave forward that sweeps enemies along. 5s cooldown.
3 / E · The Starlit Path — Stand still and place four stars with left clicks. Each is 24–230 units from the last. Then phase along the route, immune to damage, healing 35 HP per unique enemy crossed. Right click cancels planning; planning expires after 10s. 11s cooldown after travel.
4 / R · Maelstrom — Summon a whirlpool at your cursor, up to 430 units away. It lasts 5.5 seconds, pulls enemies in, and sends crashing waves outward. 20s cooldown.

Hover or focus an ability icon for its description. Howard: Q draws an armor-piercing bow with heavy knockback. Space equips stacking bleed knives for 10 seconds. E loads three footballs: aim and click to throw, gaining speed on hit. R fires triple mixed volleys automatically for 6 seconds with all effects doubled. Pause to adjust sound, effects, or the bot.
