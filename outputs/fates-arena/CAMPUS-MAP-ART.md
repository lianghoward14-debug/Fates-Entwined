# Berkeley campus pixel-art map

Campus Clash and Tidebound use a full-world pixel-art terrain render. The complete 2600×1900 campus is composited onto a half-resolution terrain canvas and enlarged with nearest-neighbor sampling, keeping deliberate square pixels crisp at gameplay zoom and on the minimap.

The map keeps the existing playable layout and adds pixel-style grass and wildflowers, shaded block canopies, sandstone walks with tile joints, a multitone Strawberry Creek and bridges, planted quads and plaza, detailed university halls, Campanile clock faces, and the twin Sather Gate towers. Both the world view and minimap share the same baked terrain.

Character movement, collision footprints, building locations, navigation, spawn points and pickups remain in their existing positions.

Open campus.html or tidebound.html and refresh the page to see the current map.
