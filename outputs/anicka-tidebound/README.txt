FATES ENTWINED — TIDEBOUND / ANICKA PROTOTYPE
15 October 2026

Double-click Play Tidebound.html. Chrome or Edge recommended.
The game is self-contained: no install, server, or internet connection needed.
Desktop keyboard and mouse required. Local bot duel only.

WASD / arrows: move. Mouse: aim. Hold left click: basic water attack.
Shift: evade. Escape: pause. Hover ability icons for descriptions.

1 / Q — SAIL ABROAD
Ride a boat for 3 seconds, steering toward your cursor. Contact damages and
pushes an enemy once per ride. Boat movement respects cover. 7s cooldown.

2 / Space — TIDAL WAVE
A broad fast-moving wave sweeps enemies forward. 5s cooldown.

3 / E — THE STARLIT PATH
Anicka stands still while you place four stars. Left click four locations,
each 24–230 arena units from the last. A circle shows the allowed distance.
After the fourth click she becomes translucent, travels the route, and is
immune to damage. She heals 35 HP per unique enemy she passes through,
up to maximum health. Travel can cross cover. If the endpoint is in cover,
she emerges at a nearby clear point. Cooldown: 11s after travel.
Right click cancels placement. Placement expires after 10 seconds.
Anicka is vulnerable while planning; immunity starts after the fourth click.

4 / R — MAELSTROM
Summons a large whirlpool at the cursor, within 430 units. After a brief
warning, it pulls enemies inward, damages them, and sends eight waves
outward every 1.1 seconds. Lasts 5.5s. 20s cooldown.

Sound starts with interaction. Sound and reduced-effects options are in the
game controls. Effects use layered synthesized audio; no downloaded music.
Howard is also selectable with his existing royal abilities.

This version adds an integrated arena HUD, four custom ability icons,
cooldown displays, boat and wake animation, star targeting, translucent
travel, layered whirlpool effects, and distinct ability audio.

Verified: engine behavior tests, browser input, four-click healing route,
pause/resume, victory/rematch, Howard, responsive layout, and console errors.
Balance and audio mix remain prototype values; no human playtest claimed.
