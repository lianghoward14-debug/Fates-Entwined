The duel

A fast, tactical fight over three zones.

Every match revolves around the Fate total in each zone. Place Supporters, consolidate them into stronger Characters, time activated effects and reactions, and win at least two zones before the match ends.

Supporters are your Reinforcement. Consolidate them into Characters with stronger Fate values and board-changing effects.

Control zones

At the end, each zone compares Fate totals. Win two zones outright, or take the match on total Fate as a tiebreaker.
