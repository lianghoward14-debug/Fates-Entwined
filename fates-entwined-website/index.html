The duel

A fast, tactical fight over three zones.

Every match revolves around the Fate total in each zone. Place Supporters, spend them to set stronger characters, use Initiator and Coordinator effects, and try to win at least two zones before the match ends.

01

Build a deck

Create a 40-card deck from cards with affiliations like Third Great War, Eventide, Expanded Worlds, and Reality.

02

Set cards

Supporters are your fuel. Bigger characters cost Supporters, but bring stronger Fate values and board-changing effects.

03

Control zones

At the end, each zone compares Fate totals. Win two zones outright, or take the match on total Fate as a tiebreaker.
