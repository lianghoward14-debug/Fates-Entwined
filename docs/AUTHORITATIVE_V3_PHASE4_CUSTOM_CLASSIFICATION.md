# Phase 4 unusual-custom-effect classification

Custom cards are eligible only when their complete rule can remain a pure
engine query, trigger, operation program, or isolated serializable handler.
Transport, room coordination, and UI code do not contain card IDs.

## Newly eligible complete rules

- **09 United Nations 5th Army** contributes two reinforcement intrinsically.
  The shared reinforcement query follows current control, remains intrinsic
  while the card's activated effects are suppressed, composes with the igb10
  landscape bonus, and is used by both legal consolidation generation and
  authoritative consolidation validation.
- **bh04 Anicka Konvicka (Selva Island)** uses a recoverable printed-type
  declaration and an atomic permanent Fate-loss operation. It preserves the
  legacy positive-integer `Math.round(20 / count)` split, excludes face-down
  and immutable targets, and records the exact target set and per-target loss.

## Explicitly deferred

- **04 Zoe** requires persistent square-origin and square-destination
  consolidation denial, plus source-leaves-play semantics.
- **28 2nd Polish-Lithuanian Army** requires a deck-origin placement command
  and player-wide once-per-turn/twice-per-game limits.
- **45 Chingachlook** remains blocked on the ambiguity ledger's interactions
  with simultaneous entry, control change, and temporary type changes.
- **78 Chaparral Hoplite** requires complete face-down public information,
  targeting, scoring, effect, and flip-command rules.
- **82 Felicyta Janowicz (Youth)** cannot change into landscapes that are not
  themselves v3 eligible and needs a serialized landscape-choice transition.
- **98 Wodny Potok Skier** requires deterministic opening-hand construction,
  an additional-card rule, duplicates, and mulligan/reconnect parity.

The remaining six stay rejected during v3 deck validation.

## Phase 4 status

Every behavior family listed in the migration plan now has at least one gated
vertical slice, but Phase 4 is not complete. The remaining cards and
landscapes must be revisited in their primary family until the eligibility
inventory reaches full rule coverage. Phase 5 must not start early.
